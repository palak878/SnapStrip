A free, elegant photo booth online — no download required

SnapStrip is a free online photo booth that runs entirely in your web browser. Instead of downloading a heavy app, you get a proper photo booth experience in one tab: choose a filter and a classic strip theme, pose for three shots, and leave with a downloadable photo strip finished in cherry, ivory, or noir.

Whether you're looking for an elegant photo strip maker for an engagement party, a refined touch for a wedding guestbook table, or simply a classy online selfie booth to send a friend, SnapStrip is built to be fast, private, and free — every photo is captured and processed on your own device, never uploaded to a server.

Why an online photo booth

Traditional photo booth machines are expensive to rent and only appear at parties. A browser-based photo booth removes that barrier: anyone with a laptop or phone camera can produce a beautifully themed photo strip in under a minute, for free, from anywhere.

Well suited to

Weddings and engagement parties, elegant corporate events, boutique retail activations, content creators who want a classic aesthetic photo strip for social media, and anyone who wants a quiet, well-made keepsake in thirty seconds.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this online photo booth really free?

Yes. SnapStrip is free to use, with no signup, no watermark, and no paywall on downloads.

Do I need to download an app?

No. SnapStrip runs entirely in your browser on desktop or mobile — there is nothing to install.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your camera feed and captured photos are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to a server.

What strip themes are available?

Classic Cherry, Ivory & Gold, Noir Cherry, and Blush — each with its own background, border, and caption styling.

Can I use this photo booth on my phone?

Yes, SnapStrip is fully responsive and works with your phone's front or rear camera.
